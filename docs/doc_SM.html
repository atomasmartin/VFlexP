Synchronous Machine (MGES & SG)

VFlexP provides two synchronous machine blocks in VFlexP/Grid/AC Generators:

  • MGES: Round-rotor or salient-pole synchronous machine (GENROU / GENSAL) equipped with an excitation system / AVR (ST4B), steam/hydro turbine governor (IEEEG1), and dual-input power system stabiliser (PSS2B).
  • SG: Synchronous machine without auxiliary controls, supporting order 8, order 4, and order 2 complexity variants.

1. Flux-Linkage Equations and Inductance Matrix

In the 6-winding representation (stator $d, q$, field $fd$, damper $kd_1$, and $q$-axis dampers $kq_1, kq_2$), the flux-current relation is:

$$ \left[\begin{array}{c} \psi_{s d} \\ \psi_{s q} \\ \psi_{f d} \\ \psi_{k d_{1}} \\ \psi_{k q_{1}} \\ \psi_{k q_{2}} \end{array}\right]= \mathbf{L} \left[\begin{array}{c} i_{s d} \\ i_{s q} \\ i_{f d} \\ i_{k d_{1}} \\ i_{k q_{1}} \\ i_{kq_{2}} \end{array}\right], \qquad \mathbf{L}=\left[\begin{array}{cc} -\mathbf{L}_{d q} & \mathbf{L}_{a d q} \\ -\mathbf{L}_{a d q} ^T & \mathbf{L}_{k}\end{array}\right] $$ $$ \left\{ \begin{array}{cc} L_{d}=L_{m d}+L_{\sigma s} \\ L_{q}=L_{m q}+L_{\sigma s} \\ \end{array} \right. $$

2. Parameter Conversion (param_genrou.m)

Standard PSS/E generator parameters $(T'_{d0}, T''_{d0}, T'_{q0}, T''_{q0}, X_d, X_q, X'_d, X'_q, X''_d, X_l, R_a)$ are converted into per-unit circuit quantities on the machine base mBase using param_genrou.m:

  • classical (default): standard series-approximation formulas assuming $T'' \ll T'$.
  • exact: IEEE Std 1110 two-circuit operational-inductance root matching, solving the coupled quadratic equations so the machine's operational inductances $L_d(s)$ and $L_q(s)$ match $(X'_d, X''_d, T'_{d0}, T''_{d0})$ and $(X'_q, X''_q, T'_{q0}, T''_{q0})$ exactly.
  • Explicit overrides: rotor circuit parameters (Lfd, Rfd, Lkd1, Rkd1, Lkq1, Rkq1, Lkq2, Rkq2) can also be overridden directly in the .dyr record.

3. Machine Complexity Levels

  • order 8 (EMT8): All 6 stator and rotor flux linkages $(\psi_{sd}, \psi_{sq}, \psi_{fd}, \psi_{kd1}, \psi_{kq1}, \psi_{kq2})$ are dynamic states, together with rotor speed $\omega_r$ and rotor angle $\delta_r$.
  • order 4 (RMS4): Stator flux derivatives are neglected ($\mathrm{d}\psi_{sd}/\mathrm{d}t = \mathrm{d}\psi_{sq}/\mathrm{d}t = 0$), leaving the 4 rotor flux states $(\psi_{fd}, \psi_{kd1}, \psi_{kq1}, \psi_{kq2})$ and $(\omega_r, \delta_r)$. Eliminating $(\psi_{sd}, \psi_{sq})$ yields the Schur-complement rotor inductance matrix $\mathbf{L}_{\text{rot}}$ and algebraic stator impedance with subtransient saliency $(L''_d, L''_q)$: $$ \begin{bmatrix} R_s & -\omega_r L''_q \\ \omega_r L''_d & R_s \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_{sd} \\ i_{sq} \end{bmatrix} = -\begin{bmatrix} v_d + \omega_r \psi_{\text{int},q} \\ v_q - \omega_r \psi_{\text{int},d} \end{bmatrix} $$
  • order 2 (SG only): Classical two-state swing model $(\omega_r, \delta_r)$.

4. Mechanical Swing Equation and Controls in MGES

The rotor swing equation is:

$$ \begin{equation} \begin{array}{ccl} T_m - T_e & = & 2H\cdot \dfrac{\mathrm{d}\omega_r}{\mathrm{d}t} + D(\omega_r - \omega_{\text{ref}}) \\ \dfrac{\mathrm{d}\delta_r}{\mathrm{d}t} & = & \omega_b \cdot (\omega_r - \omega_{\text{area}}) \end{array} \end{equation} $$

In MGES, the machine is coupled to:

  • Excitation System (ST4B): Terminal voltage transducer with time constant $T_{\text{meas}}$ (T_r), PI + lead-lag voltage regulator (K_pr, K_ir, T_a, T_b), and exciter output stage (T_e, T_f) with limits [E_fdmin, E_fdmax].
  • Turbine Governor (IEEEG1): Droop gain $1/R$ (K), lead-lag $(1+sT_2)/(1+sT_1)$, rate- and position-limited servomotor ($T_3$), and four steam turbine stages ($T_4..T_7$, $K_1..K_8$).
  • Power System Stabiliser (PSS2B): Dual-input stabiliser reading rotor speed deviation ($T_{w1}, T_{w2}, T_6$) and electrical power scaled by $1/\cos\varphi_n$ ($T_{w3}, T_{w4}, T_7, K_{s2}, K_{s3}$), ramp-tracking filter $(T_8, T_9, M=5, N=1)$, and three lead-lag stages ($T_1/T_2, T_3/T_4, T_{10}/T_{11}$) with gain $K_{s1}$.
